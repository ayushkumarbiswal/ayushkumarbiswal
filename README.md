## `01 / THE MIND BEHIND THE PRODUCT`

Hey, I'm **Ayush Kumar Biswal** 👋

B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student at **GIET University (2027)** and currently an **Intern in Transformation Consulting at Grant Thornton Bharat**, Bengaluru (Digital · dGTL).

I am interested in the intersection of **product, research, strategy, and technology**.

I start where most builds skip: the problem.

Surveys, stakeholder conversations, market research, and a lot of reading come first. I enjoy understanding users, identifying unmet needs, evaluating opportunities, and translating insights into product decisions, AI-enabled workflows, platform features, and working prototypes.

My work and explorations span healthcare, education, agriculture, accessibility, cybersecurity, and public safety.

> **Understand the people first. Challenge assumptions. Build products that matter.**

I believe a good product sits at the intersection of three things:

- **Desirability:** Does it solve a real problem for its users?
- **Feasibility:** Can it be built and implemented effectively?
- **Viability:** Can it sustain value over time?

The goal isn't simply to build something that works. It's to build something worth using.
